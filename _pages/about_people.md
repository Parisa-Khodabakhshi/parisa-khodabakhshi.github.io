## Parisa
Dr. Parisa Khodabakhshi is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering & Mechanics at Lehigh University, where she joined the faculty in August 2022. Prior to joining Lehigh, she was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Oden Institute for Computational Engineering & Sciences at The University of Texas at Austin. 

Dr. Khodabakhshi received her Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from Texas A&M University. She also earned her B.S. in Civil Engineering and her M.S. in Earthquake Engineering from Sharif University of Technology.
