We are pleased to share our new publication, *"Meshless projection model-order reduction via reference spaces for smoothed-particle hydrodynamics"*, in the Journal of Computational Physics. [This collaborative work](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021999126006741?via%3Dihub), led by **Steven N. Rodriguez**, introduces a projection-based model-order reduction framework for smoothed-particle hydrodynamics (SPH) simulations, enabling low-dimensional modeling of systems with dynamically evolving particle configurations. Congratulations to Steven and all co-authors!
