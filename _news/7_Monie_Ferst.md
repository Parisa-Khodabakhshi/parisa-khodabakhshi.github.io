I am honored to be among the invited speakers at the [**2026 Monie A. Ferst Award Symposium**](https://www.sigmaxi.org/programs/prizes-awards/monie-ferst/award-winner/j.n.-reddy), held on October 19 in Atlanta, Georgia, in honor of **Professor J. N. Reddy**. I will present our group's recent work, led by Liam K. Magargal, titled "A graph autoencoder architecture for projection-based model-order reduction." It is a pleasure to participate in this symposium recognizing Professor Reddy's contributions to computational mechanics.
